06The Craft

Slow hands,
long lives.

  1. I.

    The pattern

    Every block is drafted by hand, then refined over three fittings on real bodies — not mannequins.

  2. II.

    The cloth

    Wool from Biella, silk from Como and Garut, cashmere from Inner Mongolia. Traceable to the mill.

  3. III.

    The finish

    Hand-padded lapels, French seams, horn buttons sewn with a shank. Around 31 hours per coat.

  4. IV.

    The afterlife

    Complimentary alterations and repairs for as long as you wear it. Bring it back; we keep the pattern.
